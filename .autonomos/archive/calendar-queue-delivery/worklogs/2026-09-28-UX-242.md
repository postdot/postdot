## Scope

Queue, Compose, and Channels. Preserve the accepted brand and theme roles.
Validate long content at 1440, 820, and 390 pixels in dark and light themes.
Deliver one bounded visual proposal and a clear implementation scope; do not
invent a universal card system.

## Findings

- The live project remains sparse: two channels, one published manual
  publication, a disconnected LinkedIn destination, and no due work. No
  publication, connection, or other project data was submitted.
- At 820 px, the 36 px page title wrapped beside Compose's return action. The
  shared title now stays 30 px through tablet widths and returns to 36 px at
  1280 px and wider; “Nouvelle publication” fits on one line at 820 px. Mobile
  was already 30 px.
- Queue's selectable-list headings were 10.4 px, bold, and uppercase. They now
  use 12 px medium sentence case, consistent with `DESIGN.md`'s instruction not
  to use all-caps as the default hierarchy. The change applies only to those
  column labels.
- Sampled muted-text token contrast was 5.02:1 in light mode and 6.95:1 in dark
  mode; primary text was 14.96:1 and 17.22:1. This is a semantic-token sample,
  not a complete contrast audit. Dark surface roles are close in value (about
  1.03:1 background/canvas, 1.06:1 canvas/raised, and 1.05:1 canvas/field).
  No global surface-token change was made: the current system contract uses
  spacing and value to distinguish zones, and global token edits need evidence
  from denser populated screens before they are locked.
- The full LinkedIn label still truncates in the sidebar and authoring controls;
  tablet navigation and mobile authoring action reachability remain in UX-243.
- During Vite hot replacement after checks, the live page briefly showed a
  React route-context error (`useDashboardGlobal` outside the dashboard route)
  while `/ready` remained healthy. A full page reload recovered the route and
  the error did not recur. This is recorded as a development observation, not
  attributed to the API or treated as a confirmed production defect.

## Implementation

- Updated `DashboardPageHeader` so project page titles remain 30 px from mobile
  through tablet widths and retain their 36 px desktop size from 1280 px.
- Increased Queue's selectable-list column labels from 10.4 px uppercase to
  12 px sentence case. No new card grammar, brand, route, or semantic tokens
  were introduced.
- Kept `DESIGN.md` unchanged: the chosen changes follow its existing type-role
  and hierarchy rules. Dark surface differentiation remains a measured,
  unresolved system-level question rather than a page-local override.
