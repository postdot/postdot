# DESIGN-166 — Distinguish outside-month calendar cells

## Status

Complete.

## Owner direction

Make the leading and trailing days in the six-week Month frame easier to scan
without treating them as disabled or adding another card-like border or shadow.

## What changed

- Added a single theme-aware diagonal hatch to outside-month cells in the ReUI
  calendar.
- Derived the hatch from the existing foreground token so light and dark themes
  share the same rule without a second arbitrary palette.
- Kept the existing cell surface, interaction handlers, and accessibility
  labels unchanged.

## Evidence

- The integrated browser shows the hatch on both the leading August cell and
  trailing October cells in dark theme.
- A light-theme browser pass shows the same distinction with a light contrast
  while date cells remain readable and interactive.
- The populated calendar browser test asserts the computed repeating-gradient
  treatment.

Revisit the exact contrast only during the next owner visual checkpoint; avoid
