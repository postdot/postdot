# WEB-170 — Move the Dense Week/Day Frame Outside the Cell

- Status: Complete
- Branch: `codex/reui-calendar-spike`
- Owner: orchestrator
- Scope: ReUI Week/Day time-grid occupied-slot interaction affordance
- Related issue: ISSUE-095
- Related decision: ADR-072

## Goal

Correct the first interpretation of the dense-slot affordance. The cell must
retain its existing publication geometry; a temporary outer interaction frame
must communicate the active cell and host the create action without becoming
an additional inset surface or a literal time header.

## Evidence and direction

- Owner feedback rejected the inner framed surface because its padding consumed
  scarce calendar space and the added time label was not the important idea.
- The intended pattern is an outer envelope that appears only while a cell is
  hovered or focused and can provide an external action area.
- The frame must also work for the last horizontally scrollable column without
  clipping its action.

## Plan

1. Record the correction separately from WEB-169 and establish the external
   transient-frame contract.
2. Remove the occupied-only inner wrapper, padding, and time label while
   keeping publications as direct children of the existing slot.
3. Position the create action in a non-flowing top band of the outer frame,
   preserving the compact empty-slot baseline and keyboard reachability for
   occupied slots.
4. Verify hover/focus visibility, frame bounds, action placement, unchanged
   slot dimensions, dense content, and light/dark integrated rendering.

## Changes

- Replaced the occupied-only in-flow surface/header with an absolutely
  positioned `-inset-1` interaction frame shared by empty and occupied slots.
- The frame is visually absent at rest and appears on slot hover or
  `focus-within`; its border communicates an interaction target rather than a
  permanent card boundary.
- Moved the `+` into the frame’s external top band (`end-1`, above the slot),
  so it remains fully visible at the right edge and clears the first
  publication. No incidental slot-time text is rendered in the frame.
- Kept event cards as direct content of the slot and preserved the existing
  inspect/create distinction.

## Follow-up

Keep any future contextual actions in the external frame only when a concrete
operator need earns them; do not turn the frame into a permanent card or
reserve an action row in empty slots.
