# GOV-12 — Accept the V1 Foundation

## What Was Done

- Reviewed the three adopted V1 specifications and seven foundational decision
  records against the current product and engineering requirements.
- Accepted the records as the normative baseline for subsequent implementation.
- Kept unresolved product areas explicitly open rather than turning them into
  premature commitments.

## Key Decisions and Why

- The specifications are accepted together because the product scope, engineering
  operating model, and product foundation form one consistent boundary.
- ADR-001 through ADR-007 are accepted because they formalize choices already made
  for the domain model, runtime, persistence, scheduler, deployment topology, and
  dashboard architecture.
- Provider-specific publishing, media handling, credential details, approval policy,
  and ambiguous-outcome behavior remain governed by their dedicated tasks and
  decision records.
