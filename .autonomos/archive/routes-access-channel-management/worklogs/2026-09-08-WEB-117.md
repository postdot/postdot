# WEB-117 — Consolidate independent UX/UI audit evidence

- Date: 2026-09-08
- Status: completed
- Scope: read-only product UX/UI audit synthesis and sprint framing
- Branch: `.autonomos/archive/root-documentation-2026-10-25/source/docs/ux-ia-sprint`

## Evidence

Two independent browser audits were run against the local authenticated
application. Both exercised direct navigation and reload across the main
project routes, dark/light themes, English/French, expanded/reduced/mobile
navigation, and multiple viewport sizes. Neither auditor performed a
publication, schedule, upload, disconnect, deletion, credential change, or
provider mutation.

Muse-1.3 and Luna-max independently found no P0 in the permitted scope and
agreed that the visual foundation is coherent. Their strongest shared findings
are:

- reduced navigation needs an accessible, stable icon-only treatment;
- media upload exposes duplicate or confusing keyboard controls;
- Compose loses readable control geometry around tablet width;
- Queue tabs are truncated on narrow screens;
- notification inbox is a blocking modal rather than a contextual surface;
- provider/channel terminology and global/project settings context are unclear;
- language labels and several user-facing strings need correction.

Muse additionally identified the structural product question: Overview,
Calendar, and Queue collapse into near-identical empty states, and desktop
Calendar does not provide a clear date-oriented first action. Luna additionally
identified loading-state and global-settings navigation gaps.

## Triage

The evidence supports a dedicated UX/IA sprint rather than an isolated CSS
pass. Structural decisions must precede broad implementation. Existing
`DESIGN-97` and `DESIGN-98` remain part of the sprint; the new tasks organize
their dependencies and add the missing information-architecture, foundation,
responsive, and validation work.

## Result

Created draft [SPEC-006](../../../specs/SPEC-006-web-ux-ia-sprint.md) and the
linked issue/task plan. The specification is intentionally draft: it records
the owner's principles and the audit evidence without silently accepting the
remaining route, terminology, notification, or token decisions.
