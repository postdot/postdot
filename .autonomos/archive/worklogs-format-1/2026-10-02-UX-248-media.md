# UX-248 — Media reading modes

## Scope and decision

Continue preview calibration with the existing attached-media presentation. All
multiple attachments previously entered the same two-column cropped mosaic,
regardless of destination. Use a vertical, native-proportion presentation for
DEV.to articles and manual/custom channels: article reading does not benefit from
an invented social collage, and unknown destinations do not justify a simulated
crop. Keep image order and all attachments visible. Built-in social/message
mosaics remain unchanged pending platform-specific calibration.

This changes presentation only. It does not imply that every attached asset is
supported by the destination publisher, alter payload construction, or introduce
remote resource fetching. Attachment capability/validation remains authoritative.

cover-image concept. This pass does not invent a cover from the first attachment.
