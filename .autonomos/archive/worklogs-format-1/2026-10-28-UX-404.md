Focused error injection reproduced two different current defects:

- A failed initial draft GET set the composer ready even though its server revision and possible recovery candidate remained unknown. The form became editable and only the autosave footer reported an error; there was no explicit retry. A negative browser scenario fails on the missing Retry. Backend revision checks prevent blind overwrites, but this state leaves the operator without the loaded recovery choice and can cause avoidable revision conflicts.
- A failed recovery DELETE was swallowed after clearing local recovery. The decision closed instead of presenting its error. A second negative scenario fails on the absent error inside that decision. DESIGN.md requires recovery errors to remain visible there.

The first negative collector attempt used a nonexistent translation key for Content and failed before exercising the defect; its selector was corrected before collecting the actual read-failure baseline.

## Repair

Initial loading and failure are separate states. Pending or failed reads keep the form inert and prevent restore/discard until the server draft is known. Explicit Retry reloads it without writing or deleting either copy. Loading has visible status, and a read failure is shown outside the form or inside the existing local-recovery decision as appropriate. Trade-off: a failed initial read must recover before authoring can proceed; existing local recovery after a later autosave PUT failure remains supported.

Discard clears local recovery and closes its decision only after successful server deletion, or when a successful initial read established that no server draft exists. During deletion both decision actions are disabled. Refused deletion keeps the copy, decision and error; retry can complete it. The API, expected-revision contract, autosave queue, debounce and source/destination representation remain unchanged.
