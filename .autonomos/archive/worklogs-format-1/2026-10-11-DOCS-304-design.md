# DOCS-304 — Public surface candidate

Status: candidate, not a replacement for accepted DESIGN.md.

Compared a workspace-derived landing (familiar but risks reproducing the rejected
operational shell) with a content-first composition (stronger type, fewer surfaces,
source-to-destination adaptation as the central interaction). Choose the second
for this local review. Do not add a universal feature-card grid or decorative
motion to compensate for a weak composition.

Palette: graphite #171819, paper #f2f3f3, ink #202124, muted #a5a8af,
cobalt #5379fb, raised graphite #222427. Neutral foundations, cobalt for actionable
links and selection. Recursive Variable for body and display, with more deliberate
scale and weight than the operational pages. Generous left-aligned hero on desktop;
stack the live adaptation demonstration below it on mobile. The demo is illustrative
content with working destination selection, never a simulated publication action.

Retain native scrolling. Motion clarifies a user-requested destination change;
respect reduced motion. Documentation uses the real Fumadocs navigation, search,
table of contents and readable prose; do not redesign those affordances merely
to make the layout appear novel. Build a local static foundation, not a launch
announcement. No public GitHub/stars/download claims while the project is private.
