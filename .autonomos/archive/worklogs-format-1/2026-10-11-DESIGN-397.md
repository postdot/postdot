# DESIGN-397 — Prism across three jobs

Owner feedback: discard Signal; prefer Prism's freshness and limited palette over
Studio. Authorization is for further exploration of a whole identity, not silent
replacement of the production application or acceptance of a permanent charter.

## Pre-build direction

Audience: operators preparing and following their next publications. Domain:
source, adaptation, schedule, media, manual delivery and history. Signature:
a cool-white dispatch surface with an ink-blue bird, legible shared origin and
compact destination marks. Reject decorative glass in working controls, redundant
paragraphs, pill-shaped everything and lavender as a competing accent.

Shared primitives: canvas #f7f9fd, paper #ffffff, inset #edf1f8, ink #172641,
secondary #586781, muted #76839b, brand #3159e9. Green/amber are status-only.
Manrope for all three jobs; display tracking -.045em, normal body tracking,
4px spacing base, radius 8/12/20 by role, 40px controls (44 on touch).
Typography: landing display 88px, docs title 40px, app title 28px, body 14–17px.
Four text levels; restrained light-mode shadows, surface shifts in dark mode.

Landing: simplify the luminous stage, remove the arbitrary floating glass tile,
align material/controls with the identity. Existing landing copy remains.
Workspace: calendar-led context; no metric grid, no conversion-style headings;
shared origin and destination states live in events, queue and inspector.
Documentation: readable article-first composition with quieter navigation and
inset examples, retaining the same font, ink and control grammar.

All UI specimen controls use native elements and local sample state; no live API,
authentication, provider action or data mutation. Workspace and docs studies are
explicitly identified as specimens. Real docs remain canonical. Build, focused
browser inspection at desktop/mobile, static checks only. No app E2E matrix.

## Delivered candidate

Signal removed from active comparison; its historical route remains. Prism has
shared semantic tokens and three linked surfaces. Workspace includes calendar,
queue, local source editing, media and an in-flow publication inspector. Mobile
calendar uses an agenda instead of seven unreadable columns. Documentation is a
separate article specimen; the canonical Fumadocs pages are unchanged.

Browser inspection caught global Fumadocs heading colors leaking into the dark
specimen; explicit scoped foreground roles correct that collision. No production
app styles, API, credentials or publishing behavior changed. Theme toggles and
sample editing are local; samples are explicitly identified.

## Validation

Static export succeeds with both new routes. Focused browser checks cover both
surfaces in light/dark at 1440 and 390, no document overflow or runtime errors,
queue inspection and preservation of local text when moving between views.
Screenshots inspected for landing, desktop workspace/documentation and mobile.
No app-wide E2E run. Existing upstream ::highlight CSS warnings remain unchanged.

## Assessment and next step

The candidate establishes continuity through typography, ink/blue palette,
surface roles and geometry. It is a visual study, not a complete application or
an accepted charter. The workspace still needs production density, meaningful
navigation icons and real content states when adopted. No claim of completed
brand identity. Next: owner review across all three surfaces, then one coherent
application pass if accepted; retain Studio as comparison until that choice.
