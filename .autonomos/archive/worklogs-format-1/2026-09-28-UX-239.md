## Browser evidence retained

The authenticated French dark-theme visit covered Overview month/week/agenda,
calendar preview and publication detail, shared and channel-specific composition,
media selection and resumed composition, blank-draft validation, Queue filters,
channel creation options, media search/no-results/trash, unsupported performance,
notifications, API-key creation options, workspace groups, instance users, and
OAuth application configuration. Desktop, 820-pixel tablet, and 390-pixel mobile
layouts were inspected. Existing data were sparse: two channels, one published
manual publication, and three images. No real publication, connection, key,
invitation, or deletion was submitted. Temporary composition text/media were
removed. This is a first-impression audit, not exhaustive accessibility,
performance, dense-data, approval, or provider-delivery certification.

### Confirmed defects and observations

1. Blank-composition validation leaked into Channels under an unrelated heading.
2. Overview counted a published item as scheduled; Queue correctly counted zero.
3. The same publication used a channel label, an untitled preview heading, and a
   source excerpt on different surfaces.
4. API-key expiration showed a numeric value without days in the closed control.
5. Saved composition feedback appeared next to explicit Save draft, obscuring
   the difference between recovery of composition and publication creation.
6. Performance titles were truncated despite available space; a built-in manual
   channel was labeled as a custom execution profile.
7. Mobile preview dates and calendar period headings were truncated.
8. At 820 pixels the sidebar left approximately four month columns visible;
   mobile composition stacked substantial editor/preview height before actions.
9. Small muted operational labels competed with large headings and empty areas.
10. Channel selection and active editing shared similar controls; channel removal
    lived in delivery detail. This is an interaction-design issue, not a local
    label fix.
11. Channels repeated identity across management and connection sections;
    installation and workspace membership scopes required careful reading.
12. English notification content and built-in field/accessibility labels remained
    in the French experience. User-defined group names are not translation bugs.

### Strengths to retain

Media search and reuse were coherent; no-result copy was explicit. Composition
survived navigation with text and media. Calendar inspection remained contextual
with a direct full-detail action. Mobile automatically chose agenda. Technical
publication details were collapsed. The restrained identity can be preserved.

## Implementation and decisions

- Scope action feedback to its navigation entry, including late asynchronous
  failures; retain shared query errors as project-wide data failures.
- Count scheduled lifecycle state, not the presence of a historical timestamp.
- Reuse the existing Queue identity policy for Calendar, preview, and Performance.
- Display localized expiration labels in the closed API-key selector.
- Describe autosave as saved composition, without changing persistence behavior.
- Allow preview metadata, preview headings, calendar periods, and performance
  identity text to wrap; label built-in manual channels correctly.
- Preserve existing route and domain contracts. Larger proposals remain review
  work under UX-240 through UX-246, with explicit acceptance and dependencies.
- Append fresh evidence to existing issues rather than duplicating earlier
  design work. Reopen ISSUE-064 and ISSUE-099 for the observed remaining gaps;
  keep their prior resolution evidence as historical context.
