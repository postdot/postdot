## Transport finding changed the execution order

Before recreating credentials or publishing, checked the official tunnel documentation. [Cloudflare Quick Tunnels explicitly do not support SSE](https://developers.cloudflare.com/tunnel/get-started/quick-tunnels/#limitations). FACEBOOK-512 used a temporary account-free tunnel, so reproducing that transport is a useful prerequisite; successful local TLS acceptance does not cover this external service.

Downloaded the official Linux binary from the Cloudflare GitHub release, version 2026.10.1, into a private temporary directory and verified the asset's published SHA-256 digest. No system installation, account configuration or persistent tunnel was created. Started a loopback-only synthetic probe on port 3003 and a temporary tunnel to that probe. It exposed only synthetic transport frames and a fixed control response, with no application database, sessions, credentials or projects.

The initial minimal SSE probe delivered direct opening/data frames at once and at one-second intervals. Its HTTP control returned 200 through the tunnel in 0.281 seconds, and the SSE headers arrived in 0.141 seconds, but the tunnel delivered no frame before the ten-second read timeout.

Confirmed using the production `createProjectEventStream` function imported from `apps/api/src/modules/projects/realtime-routes.ts`, with an empty synthetic journal and one-second heartbeats. Used the route's exact response headers: `text/event-stream; charset=utf-8`, `cache-control: no-cache, no-store`, `connection: keep-alive`, `x-accel-buffering: no`. Compared a stream kept open with one aborted after three seconds. Four requests ran concurrently against the same origin process, with a six-second read timeout and no response compression observed.

| Path | First frame | Subsequent frames | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Direct, open stream | 0.008 s | 1.008, 2.009, 3.009 s | Streaming confirmed |
| Direct, three-second stream | 0.008 s | 1.008, 2.008 s | EOF at 3.008 s |
| Quick Tunnel, open stream | None | None | Headers at 0.118 s; read timed out at 6.123 s |
| Quick Tunnel, three-second stream | 3.098 s | All three frames together | EOF at 3.098 s |

The finite control proves the frames exist and survive transport but are buffered until closure. HTTP 200 and receipt of SSE headers alone do not prove event delivery. This is a measured incompatibility of this Quick Tunnel setup; it is consistent with the vendor's documented limitation. It does not prove the sole cause of the historical Facebook symptom or replace the outstanding native-provider/editor acceptance.
