## Integration failure and correction

The first unit run exposed a WAL fixture lifetime bug in wal-recovery.test.ts.
The child kept an empty interval but no live use of its Database; garbage
collection could close SQLite and checkpoint the WAL before SIGKILL. An isolated
forced-GC probe reproduced zero WAL bytes without a retained connection and
4,152 bytes with one. The fixture now retains the connection through its timer
and explicitly forces GC before its forced-stop proof. All recovery assertions
remain unchanged; the five focused WAL tests pass. No product implementation
changed to accommodate the test.
