# UX-674 focused experience review

The notification journey exercised English, French, Spanish and German at 1440
and 390 px in both themes. It used four channels, long names, grouped outcomes,
read and unread members, missing context, and failed and unconfirmed outcomes.

- IDN-01 and WAY-02: rows group one post outcome and reuse channel avatars.
- LAY-04: the page and inbox have no horizontal overflow at either width.
- FMT-01: relative labels use the locale. Absolute tooltips use the shared project
  formatter and the selected project's time zone.
- ANA-02: the inbox retains shared loading, empty, button, popover and tooltip
  components. The focused journey reports no axe violations across 16 captures.

## Unresolved observation outside UX-674

The optional French/light detail journey fails its existing mobile cancellation
hover contrast assertion. The cancellation button uses foreground `#e7000b` on
`#fde5e7`, producing 3.98:1 against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 1.4.3 requirement of 4.5:1 for its
text size. The failure occurs at the assertion after `cancel.hover()` in
`e2e/experience/detail-journeys.spec.ts`. The cancellation styling in
`apps/web/src/components/publication-lifecycle-panel.tsx` predates this change.

No SPEC-010 rule states a numeric contrast threshold. This is an accessibility
observation, not a new specification rule. The assertion remains intact. A
separate cancellation styling fix should resolve it without widening UX-674.
