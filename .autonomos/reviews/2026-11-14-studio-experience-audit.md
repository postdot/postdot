Date: 2026-11-14 (Europe/Paris)
Status: rendered review, owner-requested inventory; no code changed, no repair task opened

Request: the owner rates Studio about 3/10 and asked for an unprompted inventory of everything wrong with it, visual and experiential.

## Evidence boundary

- Rendered through the isolated end-to-end runtime (Vite development build, SQLite), Chromium, at 1440 × 900, 900 × 1100 and 390 × 844, in light and dark themes, English and French.
- Data: one owner account, two projects, six channels (LinkedIn, Bluesky, Mastodon, Discord, DEV.to, one manual), 13 posts fanned out to 33 publications across the current month, four tags, four uploaded images.
- Captures are under the untracked `test-results/owner-audit/` (about 190 files) and `test-results/experience-audit/`. They are disposable; this file keeps the conclusions.
- Not rendered: connected providers, real automatic delivery, failed or uncertain outcomes, approval requests, multi-member access, recovery mode, real touch devices, screen readers. Loading states were read from source only. Nothing here claims those.
- Findings marked _verify_ were seen once and need confirmation before repair.
- Several findings contradict rules the owner already accepted in `DESIGN.md` or SPEC-010. They are marked _accepted rule_; they are observations for an owner decision, not defects.

## Diagnosis

The individual defects below come from six causes. Fixing causes is cheaper than fixing symptoms.

1. **The interface shows the data model instead of the job.** Target, revision, profile, contract, promotion, snapshot and rebase are storage concepts. A person wants to write once, choose where it goes, choose when, and see what happened.
2. **There is no page anatomy.** Each page invents its own header, title alignment, return control, toolbar, empty state, width and button size. Nothing looks finished because nothing repeats.
3. **Channel identity is missing exactly where a multichannel product needs it.** Calendar and Queue show titles; the channel is small grey text or absent. One post to four channels is four identical lines.
4. **Navigation is scattered.** Three interchangeable sidebars, two header selectors that hide configuration links, an account menu, and settings filed under unrelated pages.
5. **Copy explains the mechanism, not the outcome.** Long helper paragraphs, engineering nouns and generic errors.
6. **Everything has the same weight.** Rare, technical and destructive controls sit beside the primary flow at equal prominence.

## What already works

Keep these; they show the intended level.

- The sign-in and setup screens: clear composition, the source-to-destinations illustration, restrained type.
- Host Grotesk, the neutral palette and the 3 px geometry.
- The Agenda calendar view: the only view showing title, channel and time together.
- The Queue grouping by post.
- The command palette structure and its footer hints.
- Notification preferences as a second pane of the popover.

## Findings

Severity: **A** blocks or misleads, **B** costs effort every day, **C** polish.

## Recommended order
