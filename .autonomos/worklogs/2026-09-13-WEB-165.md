# WEB-165 — Persist the focused calendar date and choose replacement history

## Status

Complete.

## Owner direction

Keep the calendar's temporal context across view changes and refreshes without
turning routine reading into a browser-history trail. Preserve normal history
for an explicit move into another task.

## What changed

- Added strict, timezone-safe validation and parsing for canonical civil date
  keys.
- Made `date=YYYY-MM-DD` the overview calendar's focused date for the ReUI
  Month, Week, Day, and Agenda modes.
- Preserved the focused date when switching views and when moving with
  previous/next/Today controls.
- Used TanStack Router replacement navigation for calendar reading updates and
  left Compose and full publication navigation on the default push behavior.
- Kept unrelated overview search values intact during calendar updates.
- Initialized the legacy calendar month from the same date when that temporary
  implementation is explicitly selected.

## Evidence

- The integrated Herald browser moved from Month to Week and then to the next
  period, producing `view=week&date=2026-09-19`; a reload restored both values.
- The date parser rejects malformed and impossible dates, including a non-leap
  February 29, and materializes a valid date in the project's timezone.
- Browser coverage exercises view switching, period navigation, and reload
  restoration.

## Follow-up

Keep the URL contract limited to the focused civil date. Do not persist scroll,
expanded cells, or column widths until each has a separate interaction need.
