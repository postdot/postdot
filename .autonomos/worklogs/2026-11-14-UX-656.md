# UX-656 Calendar: channel identity, grouping, stable grid, linear axis

- Outcome: Every calendar entry shows its channel marks, its time and (when not plain scheduled) a state mark; a post sent to several channels at the same minute is one entry with all its marks (`groupPublicationCalendarEvents`). Month: hairline grid, equal rows, three entries then a "+N more" popover listing the whole day (in-cell expansion removed), past days muted, outside days without hatch. Week/Day: fixed hour height, entries placed at their minute, two side by side then stacked, one-line titles. The full-height preview sheet became a compact dialog with each channel's time and state and the action in the footer. The selection bar floats instead of pushing the grid; its destructive action reads "Cancel publications". "Next publication" is strictly future. The day "+" passes a future default time. Spanish and German get their own date-fns locale.
- Decisions: ADR-103 §2/§3/§7 applied; the 2026-09-12 owner note on growing cells is marked superseded in `DESIGN.md`. Overflow popover lists the whole day, matching the contract.
- Validation: `bun run check:quick` passed; unit tests for grouping pass; focused Playwright run (calendar, overview, viewer, queue bulk, media) against the production build; `web-122-populated-states` now pins `timezoneId: 'UTC'` and a fixed morning clock because its fixtures were time-of-day dependent.
- Open: UX-661 (channel/state filter, "Move by…" reschedule). Pre-existing failures not caused here: `publication-detail-actions` local recovery dialog and `composer-account-isolation` (copy absent from the catalogs at baseline).
