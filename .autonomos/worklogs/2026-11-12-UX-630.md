Generic previews reflect manual content, shared-post headings identify their
source, and empty performance pages omit actions without reportable results.

independent page heading and management-column widths (SPEC-010 LAY-01/LAY-02),

content. Preserve pagination-aware CSV export when unloaded pages may contain
eligible results. Detail captures verify LAY-04 and retained Queue context
(WAY-03) at 390, 820 and 1920 px, in English/dark and French/light.
