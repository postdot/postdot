# DESIGN-32 — Adopt the calibrated Postbot design direction

- Date: 2026-09-01
- Status: completed
- Scope: product-wide design contract and future app/public-surface direction
- Task: DESIGN-32

## Objective

Preserve the visual direction that survived the Autonomos calibration session
before applying it to Postbot's production UI. The accepted direction must
support a full-width operational app, remain coherent across different tasks,
and leave the future landing page open as a separate composition problem.

## What was done

- Added the root [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) with the accepted provisional
  product direction.
- Added a root `AGENT.md` instruction so future user-facing UI work reads the
  contract and keeps unresolved visual evidence in worklogs.
- Recorded the H-06 identity study, H-08 palette atlas, H-09 surface grammar
  study, and H-10 multi-surface holdout as the evidence behind the contract.
- Selected `Cobalt instrument` as the provisional accent direction.
- Recorded the neutral, surface-led boundary grammar: surfaces and spacing
  first; lines only for axes, relations, controls, focus, or state; shadows
  only for meaningful layers or focal objects.
- Recorded the desktop full-width app constraint and the responsive principle
  that tablet/mobile layouts must change composition instead of shrinking the
  desktop shell.
- Kept the landing page and documentation surface as a future transfer test:
  they inherit role tokens and product truth, not the dashboard shell.

## Decisions

- The contract is accepted for Postbot but remains provisional where product
  evidence is still thin: exact navigation, contextual panel value, final type
  choice, first-run workflow, and public proposition.
- No production component or route was redesigned in this task. The contract
  is the next source of truth before that implementation work begins.
- The product identity is carried by the source-to-publications relationship,
  not by a fixed card layout, a particular border treatment, or a palette alone.

## Verification

- Owner review accepted H-10's shared identity across Calendar, Queue, and
  Compose while allowing each surface to have a different task and density.
- The temporary H-10 browser holdout was checked in dark Cobalt primary and a
  light companion; all three views switched correctly and reported no
  horizontal overflow.
- No Postbot source route or component was modified beyond the design contract,
  guidance, task record, and this worklog.
- `git diff --check` passed in the Autonomos workspace after the calibration
  record was updated.
