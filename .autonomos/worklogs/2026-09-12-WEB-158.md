# WEB-158 — Dense week/day publication slots

## Owner direction

The first Week view was not sufficiently useful: it offered no way to create a
publication from a week/day slot, and several publications sharing one time
were squeezed into fixed horizontal columns. The schedule must let the content
determine the height of a slot so all simultaneous work can be read without
inventing a cap or an overflow panel.

## What changed

- Replaced the absolute-positioned, overlap-packed Week/Day event layer with a
  flow-based time-slot matrix. Each publication starting in a slot receives its
  own stacked block, so six simultaneous publications expand that hour instead
  of becoming six narrow columns.
- Kept event blocks individually interactive for the existing in-calendar
  preview. Event content wraps its title and channel/time metadata rather than
  truncating the information into an unreadable single line.
- Made empty Week/Day slots clickable and added a hover/focus `+` affordance.
  Both use the existing Composer route and carry the slot's local date and
  exact time through validated `date` and `time` search parameters.
- Kept the Week minimum width bounded to the seven-column reading surface and
  left Day fluid. Dragging, moving, and resizing remain intentionally out of
  scope until they receive their own interaction and persistence design.
- Updated the dense ReUI browser scenario to put six publications at the exact
  same timestamp, assert that all six remain visible in Week, exercise slot
  creation at `00:00`, verify the Composer's initial datetime, and then verify
  event preview still works.

## Follow-up

Review three realistic density cases with the owner before adding interaction
complexity: several posts in one hour, posts at adjacent minutes, and a day
with both early and late scheduled work. Decide separately whether drag,
resize, or a dedicated compact/expanded event mode earns its complexity.
