# UX-660 Channels, content rules, limits and sharing

- Outcome (slice 1, audit H1–H6): Channels is one list surface. Each row shows the platform or connected account, one status word from the new shared `StatusText` (also used by `PublicationStatus`), and one next action: connect, reconnect, or "Set up LinkedIn" for an administrator (a non-administrator reads who must act). The top "One-time setup needed" banner and the setup dialog are gone. Content rules save once: Studio stores, assesses and applies in one click, and asks for confirmation only when a publication would need review. Limits show grouped numbers with units ("1,000,000 characters"), the title choice lives in one "Title" section, attachments have an on/off switch, and identity, versions, hashes and field paths are no longer shown. "Headline" is "Title" in every locale. Posting limits read as sentences ("At most _ posts per day") with a time-zone picker. The sharing dialog is one list with a remove icon per row, an "Add a project" menu, and the manager change folded into a disclosure. Telegram and custom webhook dialogs show every invalid field at once beside the field.
