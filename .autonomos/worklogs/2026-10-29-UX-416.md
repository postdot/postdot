The owner identified a regression: n8n's animated curved particle links had been replaced by inferior solid links. Restored the original DotField routing/pulse/arrival treatment in n8n with `grid="none"`, using its own component and responsive geometry. Distribution remains unchanged.

The owner explicitly rejected putting links throughout the bento, geographic map thumbnails, and interpreting their feedback as a request for simpler illustrations. The final bento uses no DotField or connection canvas:

- Projects: three independently layered content sheets (Posts, Channels with actual provider marks, Media) unfold within each folder; the folders fan out and retain illustrative per-project membership.
- Media: retain recognizable image/video files, add crop-corner motion, a video strip/playhead, and a separate preview that emerges from the original. No geographic asset or invented photo. Reduced motion keeps the composed file positions visible.
- Channels: actual provider marks and a custom module settle into a shared recessed support with sockets; the custom module lifts on hover to express extensibility. No routes.

Checks: TypeScript, targeted lint, formatting and mechanical design scan pass. Desktop/light/dark, mobile and tablet/reduced-motion captures inspected. No document overflow.
