## Prioritization review

This fixes a real command/preview mismatch, not a demonstrated data-loss defect.
The identity/execution series has reached diminishing returns for current product
progress. Further peripheral preview calibration should not automatically become
the next task. The broad authoring selection also imposes more browser coverage
than the direct causal regression needs; changing that policy remains a separate
proposal, not a reason to bypass the accepted gate during this pass.

A bounded product review should choose the next substantial user outcome from
existing capabilities and gaps, not create another exhaustive quality audit.
TASKS already records scheduling, manual/automatic delivery, media, provider
connections and deployment as implemented; EVAL-57 records a real LinkedIn image
publication. These records do not prove current complete product readiness.
Current provider profiles declare JPEG/PNG image attachments, with LinkedIn limited
to one image. Broader publication formats are a concrete candidate for product
expansion, not an approved implementation. Installation documentation, licensing
and external distribution remain separately deferred tasks.

Keep strong risk-focused verification for authorization, credential handling,
durable writes, duplicate external effects, migration and recovery. UI regressions
should protect demonstrated user behavior without multiplying every platform,
viewport and lifecycle combination for each small wiring change.
