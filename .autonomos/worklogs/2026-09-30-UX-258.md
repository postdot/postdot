## Implementation and choices

Owner feedback identified accidental removals from the 36 px tile/24 px floating close overlap, an invisible active state over opaque avatars, and a shifting channel-name row. The separate removal control retains its accessible 24 px hit target, with a small 12 px glyph, and sits farther outside the corner. On narrow screens or coarse pointers it appears only for the active destination; selecting another destination does not expose any close control over its initial tap target. Desktop fine-pointer layouts retain removal for every destination.

Removed the active channel name row, per owner feedback. Accessible names and tooltips remain; repeated platform letter cues and custom initials are preserved. Customized removal confirmation from UX-257 remains unchanged.
