## What was done

The notification projection runtime retains a ten-batch per-run budget but
continues asynchronously whenever a project reports more work. Continuations
merge project wakeups, avoid overlapping runs, and are cancelled on stop.
Transient failures schedule a delayed retry and expose `failed`/`recovering`
states instead of falsely settling on `started`.

## Key decision

Recovery stays bounded and asynchronous. It does not turn one API-triggered
projection into an unbounded synchronous loop, and production does not need a
new polling interval to drain a durable backlog after a normal start or commit
wakeup.
