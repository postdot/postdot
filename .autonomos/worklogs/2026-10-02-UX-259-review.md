## What works and what not to undo

Account remains compact, with recognizable identity and a separate security
entry. Security consequence copy earns its place in the password dialog.
Notifications distinguish severity/read state and already support loading/retry.
Performance preserves provider-specific metric meanings and cached-read versus
explicit external refresh; no synthetic score or zero should replace absent data.
Publication detail keeps technical material and activity under disclosure rather
than exposing payload/history first. Recently accepted workspace member/group
separation and distinct sidebar glyphs remain intact.

## Interpretation

The recurring weakness is task completion, not just color or border count: a
notification should lead to affected work, a metrics page should privilege
measurable results, and passive notices should not compete with a repair action.
The account dialog is the clearest bounded correction. The other surfaces need
state matrices before changes; a generic reskin would miss their operator jobs.
