# UX-690 Rebuild the image editor on the zriz engine with our own interface

Outcome: the media image editor runs on `@zriz/core` and `@zriz/react` 0.2.1 (pinned) instead of `react-easy-crop`. It adds free-angle straightening with a dial (no empty corners), flip, a free ratio and keyboard and screen reader support from the engine; saving still creates a new media item. Owner choice: zriz (<https://zriz.dev>), weighed against adopting, forking or reworking our own ([ISSUE-207](../ISSUES.md), [survey](../reviews/2026-11-18-image-editor-survey.md)).

Record:

- Engine as a dependency, interface as our code: `apps/web/src/components/media-cropper.tsx` adapts the zriz shadcn blocks (MIT, notice in `apps/web/src/components/LICENSE.zriz.md`) to Postdot's Button, Solar icons, tokens and Paraglide messages. The engine has no runtime dependency and exports in the browser; no `sharp`, no server change.
- `media-image-editor-dialog.tsx`: same props and contract. Ratios Original (default), Free, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9; the former Square, Portrait and Landscape labels became ratios because they fit on a phone and say which shape is meant. Reset appears only once something changed (engine behaviour). JPEG stays JPEG, other formats save as PNG, as before.
- Messages: straighten, flip, free, crop area, instructions and four spoken announcements in en, fr, es, de; zoom, rotation and rotate-right messages removed with their allowlist entries.
- `react-easy-crop` removed; `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and `licenses/JavaScript-dependencies.LICENSE` updated.
- Checks: new `e2e/media-image-editor.spec.ts` (landscape image cropped to 1:1 and straightened by keyboard, saved file is square and JPEG); `publication-media` editor steps updated to the radio group; `access-136-project-roles` passes. Visual check in light, dark and 390 px.

Notes:

- The editor chunk is lazy loaded (about 36 kB gzip with the engine), loaded only when someone opens the editor.
- zriz is young (0.2.1, one maintainer). If it stalls, its MIT license lets us keep the engine in the repository.
