## Implementation after owner acceptance

- Implementation base: a190fec5b; owner accepted ADR-079 on 2026-10-06.
- Added an additive Drizzle migration for narrow adoption snapshots, retaining the
  full previous working draft and any provided local destination copy. Foreign
  keys preserve referenced profiles; no old content is hidden in audit metadata.
- One transaction checks draft/source/profile identities, validates current content
  and media, writes an unselected target and media snapshot, adopts the profile,
  supersedes pending approval and records a fact. Source, selected target, jobs and
  schedule are untouched. Existing promotion remains the selection boundary.
- Read-only assessment and history routes use existing authorization. API-key
  ceilings map assessment/adoption to publication management and history to reads.
- Media usage and retention now include preserved draft/local content. A complete
  backup needs no separate service: its SQLite snapshot contains adoption history
  and its existing representation manifest includes retained bytes.
- The editor offers explicit per-binding reuse and structured editing, a validation
  action, preserved previous content, and a separate save action. Unmapped fields
  remain visible. A concurrent refusal keeps the candidate and offers a refresh.
- Historical previews read the profile owned by the selected target, independently
  from the adopted working draft. New local buffers carry their profile identity;
  mismatched or legacy buffers after adoption remain copyable, not restorable.
- No provider call, approval override, implicit migration or scheduling shortcut.
