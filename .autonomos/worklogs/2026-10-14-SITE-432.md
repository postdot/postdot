# SITE-432 — Fixed-view SVG pilot

Owner authorized proceeding to the proposed dispatch illustration. Original coordinate-based SVG source in apps/docs/design/illustrations/dispatch.py; static delivery in public/illustrations. Standalone preview at /illustrations/, not a homepage section. Three document trays, press and outgoing envelope. Single camera, neutral planes, blue accent. No browser renderer, library or animation.

Visual review exposed large-plane occlusion and cropping; fixed through explicit assembly ordering (support surface before supported objects), then expanded frame. This fixed-view engine is intentionally limited to nonintersecting ordered solids, not a general hidden-surface renderer. Do not generalize its ordering to arbitrary scenes. Both palette variants share identical geometry.

Each SVG: 23,934 bytes raw, approximately 2.2 kB gzip, 170 polygons. Native SVG accessibility title/description. Preview descriptive alt text, two themes, narrow-screen stacking. Generated assets reproduced from source. No broad tests needed for standalone art; inspected browser rendering. Scene and style remain a pilot awaiting owner judgment, not approved identity or product claim.
