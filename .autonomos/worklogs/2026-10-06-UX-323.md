## Proposal and limits

Recommend closing the ordinary approval exception first and deferring a distinct,
explicit retained-contract consent until its exact revision/assessment bindings
and execution-support checks are implemented. ADR-078 describes the alternative
of delivering both together, the preservation rules and acceptance evidence.
No approval policy, migration, role or UI was changed in this session.

The built-in registry already resolves exact execution revisions at the worker
boundary. No approval-boundary support dependency or explicit security-disable
mechanism is assumed to exist merely because the accepted specification requires
it. Those are implementation prerequisites for an exception, not reasons to allow
implicit consent today.
