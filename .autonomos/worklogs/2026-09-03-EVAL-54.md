## Blocking finding

The OAuth flow connected the existing LinkedIn credentials to the development
project successfully, but the resulting connection is identified by the
dashboard as a LinkedIn member. The provider module accepts a `memberId` and
constructs `urn:li:person:<memberId>` for both text and image publication
payloads. No organization or company-page destination is represented by the
current connection or payload contract.

Publishing now would therefore target the connected member profile rather than
the agency page used by the existing external workflow. This is a destination
identity mismatch, not a transient provider failure. No publication was
created, scheduled, or sent after the connection was established.
