# UX-677 Calendar entries per post

Outcome: In month and agenda views a post's channels share one entry for the
civil day in the project time zone, led by the earliest channel, even when a
cadence staggers them (09:00 Newsletter, 10:00 Community digest is one entry).
Week and day views keep same-minute grouping because their position is a time.
Each entry carries its channel marks, an explicit state icon (failed: warning
triangle, manual publication due: clipboard check, all published: check circle;
scheduled has none), its time when the cell has room, and its title.

Hover shows our own card (the browser `title` tooltip is gone): the post title,
each channel with its time and state in words, and a one-line hint that a click
previews and a right click opens actions. Right click opens a context menu:
Preview, View post, mark as published (per due channel), retry (per failed
channel), Select. Viewers get Preview and View post only. The day "+" is a quiet
ghost button with our tooltip instead of a filled primary square.

Decisions: The state icon replaces the coloured bar the owner could not read.
In narrow month cells the time hides so the title keeps its room; the hover card
gives every time. The context menu reuses the dropdown menu items through a new
shared `ContextMenu` in `packages/ui`. ADR-103 §3 already asks for one entry per
post; no decision record change.

Validation: unit test for day grouping across time zones; `bun test src/lib`
(301 pass); typecheck and lint; e2e calendar-filter-move, channel-shared-calendar,
design-121-shell, web-122 empty and populated states, web-124 outcome states,
web-310 viewer actions: 29 passed after updating two empty-value assertions
from the dash removal. Showcase captures in `test-results/owner-audit/cal2`.
