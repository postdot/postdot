## Recommended CONTENT-370 boundary for owner approval

Offer an explicit action to add destinations to an existing post. The new
publication starts as a draft and refers to an explicitly identified source
revision. Do not reschedule or modify existing publications, execution snapshots,
media order, approvals, or historical targets. Existing destination adaptations
are not silently propagated. Reusing the source is distinct from duplicating a
publication into a new post.

Prefer the current source revision as the initial choice, clearly identified;
using historical publication content is a different operation. Establish whether
multiple publications for the same channel are permitted before implementing the
request contract. The report does not decide that policy or introduce a source
revision migration. Inspect current source mutation/history ownership first.

Credible alternatives are separate new posts or the existing draft duplication.
They avoid a new source management surface but lose the explicit shared-post
relationship when another destination is added later. The recommended outcome
makes that relationship useful beyond initial creation. It requires owner scope
approval because source ownership and historical guarantees are consequential.

First implementation acceptance should prove the new destination is a draft of
the chosen source revision, existing publications remain unchanged, and invalid
or unauthorized additions leave no partial records. Use focused domain/API tests
and one browser journey; broader checks follow ADR-077 at the integration point,
not repeatedly during iteration.
