# UX-689 Reschedule publications by dragging them in the calendar

Outcome: in the project calendar, a scheduled entry can be dragged to another day (month view) or time (week and day views). A post sent to several channels moves as one entry and every channel keeps its offset. A success toast names the new time and offers Undo, as other one-click state changes do (ADR-103 §6).

Record:

- `apps/web/src/components/publication-calendar.tsx`: drag enabled, resize and slot selection still off (a publication is a moment, not a span). An entry is draggable only when every publication in it is `scheduled` and the viewer can edit; drafts, published, failed, cancelled and other projects' occupancy stay fixed. `canDropEvent` refuses a time in the past. The dropped entry is shown at its new time while the move is saved, then the refreshed list takes over; a refused move puts it back.
- `apps/web/src/components/publication-calendar-page.tsx` passes the existing `reschedulePublications` action (same API and cadence confirmation as the bulk "Move" dialog) with one time per publication.
- Checks: new `e2e/calendar-drag-reschedule.spec.ts` (two-channel post moved by a day with its 15-minute offset, undo restores both, a drop on a past day changes nothing); `publication-media`, `channel-shared-calendar`, `design-121-shell` and `access-136-project-roles` pass against the production build.

Notes:

- Competitor survey: drag and drop is a baseline expectation (`.autonomos/reviews/2026-11-18-competitor-survey.md`).
