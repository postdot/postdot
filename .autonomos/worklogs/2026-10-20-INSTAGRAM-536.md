## Current provider evidence

Read through the T3 preview on 2026-10-20:

- Business Login guide (updated March 13, 2026): authorize at `https://www.instagram.com/oauth/authorize`; confidential POST form code exchange at `https://api.instagram.com/oauth/access_token`; response documents one `data` item with access token, app-scoped `user_id` and comma-separated granted permissions. Long-lived exchange is GET `https://graph.instagram.com/access_token` with `ig_exchange_token`, app secret and short-lived token. Response includes bearer token and actual `expires_in`. Refresh is GET `/refresh_access_token` with `ig_refresh_token` and a valid token at least 24 hours old. An expired token cannot be refreshed. No OAuth refresh-token string is documented.
- The selected confidential flow documents secret/state and no PKCE parameters. Keep any provider-specific compatibility handling isolated and explicit; never change the shared default. The exact callback/state and application/project/actor attempt binding remain mandatory.
- The guide documents only a literal `#_` callback fragment artifact; do not accept arbitrary fragments, mismatched callbacks or duplicate state/code parameters.
- Get Started guide: Graph v26.0 `/me` exposes `id` (app-scoped), `user_id` (professional publishing account), `username` and `account_type` (Business or Media_Creator). The sample wraps the account in `data`. Request both IDs deliberately; do not confuse the app-scoped token identity with the publishing destination. No personal-account support.
- Required scopes remain only `instagram_business_basic` and `instagram_business_content_publish`. Missing grant metadata does not establish permission. Standard Access applies to eligible owned/managed accounts added to the app; serving other businesses requires Advanced Access. Neither is proven by synthetic tests.
