# UX-691 Lighten the image description popover

Outcome: the ALT popover holds a short label ("Alt text"), the field and a two-word placeholder, with padding around them. Resolves GH #51 (owner review of the popover added on 2026-11-18).

Record:

- `AccessibilityTextBadge` in `media-attachment-picker.tsx`: the explanation line under the field is gone; the popover gets `p-3` (the shared `PopoverContent` has no padding of its own, which is why the field touched the border) and a little more width.
- Messages in en, fr, es, de: `media_accessibility_text` is now "Alt text" and its translations, wherever it is used (popover, media card, information dialog, recovery comparison); the placeholder is "Describe the image"; `media_accessibility_hint` removed.
- Checks: `editor-composer-pilot`, `web-124-outcome-states` and `publication-media` pass against the production build; screenshots at 390 px in light and dark.

Notes:

- My first version over-explained a field everyone recognizes and was reviewed as cramped; lesson for later popovers: check `PopoverContent` padding, and do not explain a standard field.
