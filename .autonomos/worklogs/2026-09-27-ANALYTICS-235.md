- Scope: A localized project-level view of cached provider observations with explicit refresh controls.

## Accepted boundaries

- Keep provider-owned publication performance distinct from Postbot operational analytics.
- Display provider-specific measures per published destination; do not aggregate incomparable metrics across networks.
- Support existing Bluesky observations and explicit DEV.to/Forem lifetime-total collection.
- Page reads use persisted observations only and never contact publishing providers.
- Show unsupported providers and unavailable/uncollected metrics honestly; preserve valid zero values.
- Do not introduce scheduled polling, historical charts, new credentials/scopes, or retention changes.

## Implementation and verification
