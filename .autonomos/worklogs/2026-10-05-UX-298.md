# UX-298 — Destination body preview selection and locked revision consistency

First measured product mission under [ADR-077](../../../decisions/ADR-077-two-validation-obligations.md).
Presentation only: no source ownership, transformation, persistence, promotion,
scheduling, or publisher contract changed, and no real provider was contacted.

## What was wrong

`getTargetPreviewText` in `apps/web/src/components/publication-editor.tsx` guessed
the destination body from top-level property names:

```ts
for (const key of ['commentary', 'text', 'body', 'body_markdown', 'content']) {
```

Three consequences, all real:

1. **A nested binding was missed.** A profile declaring `primary_text` at
   `/article/body` produced no guess, so the preview fell back to the *source* body.
2. **A custom profile path was missed** for the same reason, unless it happened to
   be called `body`, `text`, `commentary`, `body_markdown`, or `content`.
3. **The guess could read the wrong field.** A destination document with an
   unbound top-level `content` string would be previewed as the body even when the
   declared field was absent.

The built-in presets all bind `primary_text` at a top-level path
(`/text`, `/commentary`, `/body_markdown`, `/body`), which is exactly why the
guess survived as long as it did.

The headline had already been corrected by UX-297 to read the declared `headline`
binding through `getTargetEditorFields` and `readTargetField`. The body had not.

## Second gap: a locked publication read two revisions

UX-297 made locked *inspection* read `editor.selectedTarget` for the headline, but
the body and media did not:

- `previewContent` read `targetResult.value`, the working draft;
- `previewMediaSelections` read `targetResult.value`, the working draft.

So for a lifecycle-locked publication carrying a newer, not yet applied draft, the
preview showed the **selected execution** headline beside the **working draft** body
and media. Inspection is a statement about what will be or was delivered; mixing
revisions makes it say something that never existed.

## Correction, reusing the existing mechanism

- `getTargetPreviewBody(profile, target)` reads the field declared `primary_text`,
  through the same `getTargetEditorFields` + `readTargetField` pair the headline
  already uses. `getTargetPreviewTitle` and the new reader now share one private
  helper, so the two semantics cannot drift apart.
- `getPreviewTarget({ editable, workingTarget, selectedTarget })` is the single
  selector for which destination document the preview reads. Title, body and media
  all take it, so the same-revision guarantee holds by construction rather than by
  three expressions happening to agree.
- The key-guessing helper is deleted, not left unused.

Preserved deliberately:

- **Intentionally empty adaptations stay empty.** A declared field holding `''` or
  whitespace previews as itself; nothing fills it in.
- **Source/destination separation.** When a destination document exists but declares
  no `primary_text`, the preview shows no body rather than substituting source
  content. When no destination document exists at all, the source body is still what
  the destination will carry, so that case is unchanged.
- **Publication rules.** Locked publications are inspected, not edited; the
  selector reads the selected execution target and never mutates either it or the
  working draft.

## Verification

`apps/web/src/lib/publication-preview.test.ts`, 15 passing, adding six cases for the
body reader and four for the selector:

- nested `/article/body` binding beats a decoy top-level `content` string;
- a custom top-level path (`/messageBody`) beats the preset key;
- an absent declared field yields nothing rather than the decoy;
- blank and whitespace adaptations are preserved;
- a missing document, an unbound profile, and a non-textual declared field all
  yield nothing;
- while editable the working target is read, once locked the selected target is,
  and with no selection the working target is the fallback;
- one selector feeds headline, body and media, so they cannot disagree.

## Strengthened locked inspection

The existing locked-inspection scenario already gave the later draft a different
headline. It now also gives it a different **body and different media**, created as
two real assets through the existing reserve, upload, finalize API, so no UI
journey was duplicated. The scenario asserts, before promotion and after locking:

- while editable, the preview follows the working draft on all three inputs, and
  shows the later draft's asset, not the selected one;
- once locked, the preview shows the selected headline, the selected body, and the
  selected asset, and shows neither the later body nor the later asset.

That is the discriminating case the earlier version of this mission left untested:
headline, body and media all had to come from one revision.

Scope of this strengthening: `e2e/editor-composer-pilot.spec.ts`, one scenario,
run alone in 21.5 s, passing. The assertions describe already-correct behaviour
after the correction in this mission, so they were not re-run against the unfixed
code.
