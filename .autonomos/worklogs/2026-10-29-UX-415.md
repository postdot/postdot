# UX-415 — Authentication pages and onboarding, first pass

Status: in progress (awaiting owner review of the first direction)

## Audit

`AuthShell` was the common template: a saturated blue panel with translucent squares and a floating logo beside a small form. It contradicted DESIGN.md (no coloured foundations, no decoration without a job, Cobalt as a signal) and failed the subject-swap test. Light-mode fields (#F4F4F4 on #EAEAEA) were barely distinguishable, `setup` explained first-run consequences in one paragraph, and the mobile layout floated the form in empty space. Screenshots of the five pages (light/dark, desktop/mobile) were taken before and after.

## Direction taken (owner delegated the first proposal)

- Neutral page foundations (#F4F4F4/#171717); no coloured panel. Brand lockup top-left without the framed tile.
- Desktop-only static "dispatch board": one source post connected to four destinations, each with its own publication state (existing state labels and tokens), plus a one-line caption. Decorative-free and animation-free; hidden from assistive technology as an illustration. Sample destination names are proper nouns; the source title and caption are in the message catalogs (en/fr).
- Fields use the raised surface with the system border and primitive corners (the local `rounded-lg` overrides were removed). Heading uses tighter tracking.
- `setup` gets a short numbered "what happens next" list (administrator, signup closes, invite others); its description is shortened accordingly. `AuthForm` gains an optional `beforeForm` slot.
- Forgot/reset/invite pages inherit the shell and field changes. Authentication logic is untouched.

## Validation

- `bun run check:quick` passes (typecheck, i18n coverage 2,189 messages, lint, spelling, format).
- Ten setup, password-reset (online/offline), invitation and session-renewal journeys pass; accessibility-smoke and anonymous-loading results are in the combined log.
- Visual review of login and setup in light/dark desktop and light mobile only; the other captures were generated but not individually inspected. No physical-device acceptance.

## Limits and next scope

Logo is unchanged and will be a drop-and-replace by the owner. The board is a first proposal; owner feedback or references may redirect the composition. The invitation page's own states (valid invitation, accepted, mismatched account) keep their layout inside the new shell and were not redesigned.

Recommended next step: owner review of the first direction; then refine and extend to the invitation states.
