# UX-415 — Authentication pages and onboarding, first pass

Status: in progress (v1 tagged; v2 awaiting owner review)

## Audit

`AuthShell` was the common template: a saturated blue panel with translucent squares and a floating logo beside a small form. It contradicted DESIGN.md (no coloured foundations, no decoration without a job, Cobalt as a signal) and failed the subject-swap test. Light-mode fields (#F4F4F4 on #EAEAEA) were barely distinguishable, `setup` explained first-run consequences in one paragraph, and the mobile layout floated the form in empty space. Screenshots of the five pages (light/dark, desktop/mobile) were taken before and after.

## Direction taken (owner delegated the first proposal)

- Neutral page foundations (#F4F4F4/#171717); no coloured panel. Brand lockup top-left without the framed tile.
- Desktop-only static "dispatch board": one source post connected to four destinations, each with its own publication state (existing state labels and tokens), plus a one-line caption. Decorative-free and animation-free; hidden from assistive technology as an illustration. Sample destination names are proper nouns; the source title and caption are in the message catalogs (en/fr).
- Fields use the raised surface with the system border and primitive corners (the local `rounded-lg` overrides were removed). Heading uses tighter tracking.
- `setup` gets a short numbered "what happens next" list (administrator, signup closes, invite others); its description is shortened accordingly. `AuthForm` gains an optional `beforeForm` slot.
- Forgot/reset/invite pages inherit the shell and field changes. Authentication logic is untouched.

## Validation

- `bun run check:quick` passes (typecheck, i18n coverage 2,189 messages, lint, spelling, format).
- Ten setup, password-reset (online/offline), invitation and session-renewal journeys pass; accessibility-smoke and anonymous-loading results are in the combined log.
- Visual review of login and setup in light/dark desktop and light mobile only; the other captures were generated but not individually inspected. No physical-device acceptance.

## Limits and next scope

Logo is unchanged and will be a drop-and-replace by the owner. The board is a first proposal; owner feedback or references may redirect the composition. The invitation page's own states (valid invitation, accepted, mismatched account) keep their layout inside the new shell and were not redesigned.

Recommended next step: owner review of the first direction; then refine and extend to the invitation states.

## Second direction (v2, owner asked for a bolder pass)

Owner feedback on v1: clean and better than before, but too safe. Asked for v1 to be versioned first (git tag `auth-redesign-v1`, commit 91150a39e) and for a second, riskier version, drawing on the Hack Templates gallery (MIT, the saoudi-h gallery repository) where it fits the art direction.

Borrowed, adapted: monochrome dot-matrix/halftone fields, a single coloured signal cutting through a neutral field (Signal), film grain, bold/light weight contrast inside one family (no new typeface), pointer-reactive field that pauses off-screen and respects reduced motion. Not borrowed: colour gradients, WebGL, display type, dark-forced panels (DESIGN.md prohibits coloured foundations; the stage follows the light/dark theme).

- New `auth-dispatch-stage.tsx`: Canvas 2D dot field behind the real source/destination cards. Each route is a dotted line (Cobalt token) from the source to a destination; a pulse travels each route in turn and the arrival ripples across the field in that destination's own state colour (published, queued, needs review, draft). Pointer brightens and pushes nearby dots. Grain overlay, headline "One source. / Every destination." in weight contrast.
- Behaviour: pauses when hidden or off-screen, one static frame with reduced motion, re-reads colours on theme change, recomputes layout on resize, DPR capped at 2. Hidden from assistive technology. Desktop only (lg and up); mobile keeps the compact form.
- DESIGN.md says motion needs an explicitly discussed interaction; the owner discussed it in this conversation. The exception is scoped to the authentication stage; DESIGN.md is not yet amended pending owner acceptance of the direction.

Validation: `check:quick` passes; installation-setup (en/fr), accessibility-smoke, anonymous-loading, workspace-invitation, offline password reset and session-renewal journeys pass (13). Visual review in dark 1440, light 1440 and a 1024 layout measurement. Online password-reset script not rerun (the stage is decorative and untouched by it).

## v2 owner review and polish

Owner review: v2 approved as a large perceived-quality gain (animation fluidity, duotone headline, pointer effects). Two corrections applied: destination chips now show brand logos instead of names alone, and the form column is centred. Logos reuse `@iconify-icons/logos` (Mastodon, Bluesky, LinkedIn, DEV) rather than `ChannelAvatar`, which would pull DiceBear into the anonymous login bundle; the production anonymous-loading budget test still passes. The fourth destination changed from "Newsletter" (no brand mark) to DEV. Validation: check:quick; setup en/fr, accessibility-smoke and invitation journeys pass.

Follow-up recorded as UX-416: apply the same motion language to the postdot.app landing page.
