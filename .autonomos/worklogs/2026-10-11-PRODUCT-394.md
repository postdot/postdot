## Proposed DOCS-301 pass

Include operational boundaries for backups, recovery holds, updates and stale locks; do not implement automatic backup scheduling or claim an upgrade path that has not been tested.

Success is a reproducible first installation, not document volume or another round of exhaustive tests. Licensing, public hosting, registry publication, architecture acceptance and a marketing site stay separate.
