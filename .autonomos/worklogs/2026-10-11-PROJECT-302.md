# PROJECT-302 — AGPL adoption and launch preparation

## Accepted direction

The owner explicitly selected AGPL-3.0-only on 2026-10-11. Herald remains private
until public communication and distribution preparation are ready. No hosted
service, proprietary edition, commercial dual license or public opening is
implemented or authorized by this pass. ADR-086 records the accepted decision.

## Implemented

- Official unmodified GNU AGPL version 3 text downloaded from
  https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.txt into root LICENSE.
- SPDX license field in the root and all 17 private workspace manifests. Their
  private flags remain unchanged; package publication is not enabled.
- README license section and contribution terms: original contributions under
  AGPL-3.0-only, authors retain copyright, no assignment or commercial relicensing
  grant requested. Third-party material retains its licenses.
- Docker packaging includes LICENSE and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md. This does not
  imply complete clearance of every shipped component.
- Inventory of 79 direct production dependency declarations from installed
  versions. Resolver fallback reads workspace package manifests when exports
  prevent entry-point resolution. Twitter text has no declared license field;
  its actual LICENSE establishes Apache-2.0. No version or lockfile changes.
- Explicit notes for font OFL, imported DiceBear CC0 definitions, adapted shadcn,
  platform symbols, MP4Box and the separate FFmpeg executable. The inventory is
  not a transitive bundle audit or a legal compatibility certificate.
- Proposed public positioning in docs/project/public-positioning.md: independent
  maintainers and small teams; one source with independent destination adaptations;
  manual and automated destinations together. Four demonstration paths and bounded
  claims, no promise of universal providers or supported architectures.

## Remaining release preparation

The owner selected their own name for copyright; exact public spelling was
requested rather than inferred from local machine/account data. No invented
copyright holder is written. The GNU copyright in LICENSE refers to the license
text, not ownership of Herald.

Collect full notices for actual bundled transitive code and fonts, verify the
provenance/permissions of empty-state SVGs and the sample portrait, and establish
corresponding-source availability and suitable user-facing source/legal notices
before public distribution. The portrait's old worklog only identifies an
owner-supplied Unsplash image, not an exact source URL. Do not treat absence of
provenance as proof of either permission or infringement.

PROJECT-302 remains in progress for these checks. DOCS-301 can follow once the
owner approves the public positioning; no duplicate installation task added.
Do not publish source, images or website without explicit authorization.

## Validation

(17 typecheck tasks, about 22 seconds). Lockfile-only install reported no changes.

container verified both packaged files by SHA-256 against their repository copies. No unit or browser matrix
was run for metadata and documentation changes. Existing private data, providers,
services and unrelated untracked paths were not modified.
