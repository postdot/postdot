---
title: Posts and destinations
description: One shared source. Independent publications.
---

## Post

A post holds the shared source: title, text and media. You can update the source
without rewriting destination adaptations or changing past publications.

## Publication

Each destination has its own publication, draft, schedule and execution history.
A LinkedIn adaptation and a Bluesky adaptation can come from the same post while
carrying different text and media.

Applying a source update to a destination is an explicit review action. Saving the
source does not propagate changes automatically or publish anything.

## Channel and publisher

A channel describes a destination and its content requirements. A connected
publisher enables automated delivery. A manual channel works without one.

Channel connections belong to the project, rather than the member who connected
them. Access to the project and permission to edit remain separate decisions.

## Delivery state

Draft, scheduled, published and failed are different states. An ambiguous outcome
means the provider result is uncertain; check it before deciding to retry. A saved
editor state does not mean the destination has been published.
