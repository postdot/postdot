title: Your first publication
description: Try the complete workflow without a provider account.
---

## Create a project

After installation, register the first administrator account and create a project.
A project holds its channels, posts, media and member access.

## Add a manual channel

Open **Channels**, add a channel and choose a manual profile. It needs no external
credentials. A manual channel is a destination whose delivery you complete yourself.

## Write a post

Open **New publication**, write the shared source and choose your channel.
Save a draft to inspect the post and its destination without publishing.

Open that destination to review its own content. When ready, use the manual
publication workflow to record the result after you publish it externally.

## Connect an automated destination

Add a supported provider channel and connect its account from **Channels**.
Review the destination requirements before scheduling. A working connection alone
does not guarantee that every piece of content meets the provider's constraints.

The **Queue** shows publication state and pending human work. **Overview** shows
publication timing. Neither changes a saved draft simply because you visit it.
