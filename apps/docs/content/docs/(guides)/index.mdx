Postbot brings a shared source post and its destination publications into one
workspace. Adapt the content for each channel, schedule automated delivery, or
track the work you publish manually.

Start with [installation](/docs/installation/), then create your
[first publication](/docs/first-publication/).

## Use Studio

- [Posts and destinations](/docs/concepts/) explains shared content and independent versions.
- [Provider connections](/docs/providers/) covers connected channels and authorization.
- [Media](/docs/media/) explains originals and lightweight previews.

## Other documentation spaces

- [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting/) covers installation, storage, backups and updates.
- [Development](/docs/development/) covers clients, integrations and the codebase.
- [API reference](/docs/reference/) points to your instance's OpenAPI contract and authentication rules.
