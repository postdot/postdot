- Prompt/request: transfer the accepted H-10 Herald identity to a landing page without reusing the dashboard shell
- Product truth: Herald is a self-hosted multichannel publication orchestrator; one Post fans out to destination-specific Publications connected to Channels; delivery can be automatic or manual

- Viewport/device: 1280 × 900, pointer; narrow companion checks at 768 × 900 and 375 × 900
- Contract under test: H-10 accepted direction — Cobalt instrument, neutral surface-led grammar, source-to-publications signature
- Open product decisions: final public audience, proposition, proof assets, and conversion action remain provisional

## Candidates

- Dispatch narrative: asymmetric hero with a source object and branching destination publications as the focal proof
- Publication ledger: editorial claim with a vertical publication ledger and sequential explanation below

## Rubric

Hard gates:

- [ ] The reader can identify Herald's product category and first action without the lab controls.
- [ ] The landing composition does not reproduce the authenticated dashboard shell.
- [ ] The source-to-publications relationship is visible with real product vocabulary.
- [ ] The rendered page is checked at wide, tablet, and narrow widths without horizontal overflow.

Observable criteria:

- [ ] The page has a proposition rather than a decorative hero.
- [ ] The identity survives a marketing composition: Cobalt remains a signal, foundations remain neutral, and surfaces do not become a card grid.
- [ ] The relationship graphic feels specific to publication orchestration rather than a generic SaaS workflow.
- [ ] Automatic and manual delivery are understandable without invented metrics or filler content.
- [ ] The two candidates are meaningfully different in structure, not just palette or type.
